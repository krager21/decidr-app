# Privacy Policy for Decidr

**Last updated: July 2026**

## Overview

Decidr ("the App") helps you decide what to do right now by dealing you activity suggestions as cards. This policy describes what data the App handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. The short version: everything about you lives on your device; your location leaves it only when you switch on an optional feature that needs it, and even then it is sent only to the services below and never stored by us.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored locally on your device and never uploaded by us:

- **Preferences** — environment, mood, energy level, weirdness, time of day, social context, duration, interests, and any saved preference profiles
- **Activity history** — activities you've marked as done
- **Favorites and custom cards** — activities you've hearted or added yourself
- **Feedback** — cards you've skipped or disliked, used to tune future deals

If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted. We operate no account system and no server of our own.

## Optional Features That Use Your Location

Both features below are **off by default** and require you to turn on "Use my location" in Settings → Personalization. When enabled:

- **Weather-aware suggestions** — your device's approximate coordinates are sent to **OpenWeatherMap** (openweathermap.org) to fetch current conditions. Subject to the [OpenWeatherMap privacy policy](https://openweathermap.org/privacy-policy).
- **Nearby places** (Premium) — your device's coordinates are sent to an **OpenStreetMap Overpass API** server (overpass.kumi.systems or overpass-api.de) to find places around you. Subject to the operators' policies ([OSMF privacy policy](https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy)).

In both cases the coordinates are used to answer that one request. We do not store, log, or share your location, and the App never accesses location in the background. Turning the toggles off stops all location use immediately.

## Purchases

Decidr Premium is offered as an in-app purchase processed by Apple's App Store or Google Play and managed through **RevenueCat**, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives purchase receipts and an anonymous app-generated identifier — never your name, email, or location. See the [RevenueCat privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/). We do not see your payment details.

## What We Don't Do

- No personal information collected (name, email, phone number)
- No usage analytics or tracking technologies
- No advertising
- No selling or sharing of data with third parties beyond the request-scoped services listed above

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
